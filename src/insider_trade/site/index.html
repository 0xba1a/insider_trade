How the score works

Only open-market purchases (Form 4 code P) by officers and directors with a trade date in the window count. 10% owners and funds, grants, option exercises and sales are ignored.

  • Cost: average percentile of total $ bought, $ bought as % of market cap, and ΔOwn (shares bought ÷ shares held before the first buy, capped at 100%).
  • Recurrence: percentile of distinct buy days + insiders who bought on 2 or more days.
  • Insiders: percentile of distinct insiders weighted by role (CEO/CFO/President 3, other officers 2, directors 1.5).

Form 4 also codes IPO and private-placement purchases as P, so newly listed companies can rank high. Prices come from Yahoo Finance and are unofficial. Not investment advice.
